## 1. Narrative Spine

**Audience:** Fire TV track judges, in a video under three minutes.

**Hook:** You are mid-squat in the living room. The video on the TV cannot see that your knees caved in.

**Turn:** The TV marks the knees and says one fix. You correct. The marker changes. The set ends and rest starts without touching the remote.

**Ask:** Score it as a computer-vision fitness demo that keeps the camera picture in the room.
